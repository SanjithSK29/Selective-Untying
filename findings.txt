1. MAIN EXPERIMENT
Four methods x 12 paired SFT seeds (0–11), one pretrained checkpoint,
the same 500 qualifying validation prompts. Source: results_v2.json.
The extension followed inspection of the initial three-seed results.

conflict       33.6833           70.5392           1.5097250

Conflict-minus-baseline satisfaction (percentage points), paired 95% t intervals:
frequency:    -0.0333 [-0.9168, +0.8501]
freq_random:  -0.1167 [-0.9065, +0.6731]
tied:         +0.1500 [-0.6630, +0.9630]

The targeted >=2pp gain over both selection baselines is not observed.
Conflict NLL minus tied = +0.0014083 [0.0013207, 0.0014959], also failing
the no-NLL-degradation guardrail. Lower NLL is better.

These intervals are conditional on the checkpoint, prompt sample, calibration
and one fixed frequency-matched-random token set. They do not establish universal
equivalence. Repeated use of 500 prompts is not 6,000 independent prompts.
All four methods' seeds 0–2 exactly reproduce historical satisfaction/NLL.

4. METRICS AND HUMAN EVALUATION
Satisfaction: all requested words present, case-insensitive whole-word matching.
Macro recall: average per-prompt fraction of requested words present.
NLL: token-weighted teacher-forced reference-response prediction loss.
None directly measures generated-story coherence or semantic instruction following.

No blinded human coherence/integration ratings were conducted or supplied.
No human scores are claimed. Full generated texts/final SFT checkpoints were not
saved by the historical scripts; new runs save them for future audits.

6. CONCLUSION
Conflict-guided selection fails the specified success criterion in this small-model,
keyword-constrained story task. The 12-seed full-set analysis and 371-prompt
post-hoc non-overlap sensitivity analysis do not support a +2pp gain over the
selection baselines. Larger models/tasks and other calibration objectives remain
untested. See README, reports/main, reports/exploratory and docs/EVALUATION.md.
